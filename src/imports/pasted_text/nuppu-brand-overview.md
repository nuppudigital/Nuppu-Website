Background Idea
Nuppu Digital responds to the growing need to support children’s emotional skills at an early
stage of development. In everyday life, children encounter emotions and stress that they are
not yet able to put into words. Nuppu offers children a safe, gentle, and encouraging way to
explore their own emotions before challenges begin to pile up.
The foundation of the brand is the idea that understanding emotions is a skill that can be
practiced with the same lightness as learning to read or count.

Founder Story
Emmi Yli-Sissala, Founder
I am a registered nurse and the mother of two toddlers. At home, I have experienced firsthand
how few concrete tools parents often have to support children’s emotional skills in everyday
life.
As a nurse, I believe in prevention. As a mother, I know that practicing emotional skills begins
already in early childhood. Based on these insights, Nuppu Digital was born: a gentle, child-
centered emotional education ecosystem that supports both children and parents in everyday
life.
Nuppu is not just an app—it is a response to a real need to provide emotional skills early,
safely, and equally to all children.

Brand Promise
At the core of the Nuppu Digital brand is emotion, not performance. It does not require the
child to be anything other than who they are, but meets them exactly where they are in the
moment.
The brand promise is clear and strong: emotional education can be fun, safe, and easily
accessible for all children. Nuppu builds trust with both children and adults by being warm,
thoughtful, and genuinely on the child’s side.

Visual Promise

Nuppu’s visual world is designed to calm rather than overstimulate. Soft, rounded characters
are empathetic and approachable, serving as mirrors of emotional states for the child.
Pastel-toned, slightly hand-drawn illustrations bring a sense of humanity and distinguish
Nuppu from hard, technical learning solutions. The visual identity supports focus, a sense of
safety, and long-term use, which is also essential for product engagement.

User Experience for Children
The child’s user experience is built around emotions. Navigation does not ask what the child
can do, but how they feel. The interface is clear and intuitive, allowing the child to use it
independently without constant guidance.
Small, positive feedback reinforces a sense of success and supports intrinsic motivation. The
experience is designed so that the child wants to return to Nuppu again and again.

Value for Parents and Teachers
For adults, Nuppu offers support, tools, peace of mind, and clarity. Parents and teachers
receive a calm overview of the child’s progress without constant notification overload or
demanding usage.
Nuppu does not add to everyday burden but supports what adults are already doing for the
child’s well-being. This makes the product easy to adopt and a natural part of family and
school life.

Technology Supporting Children’s Emotional Development
Nuppu Digital uses technology thoughtfully and with respect for the child’s development.
Technology acts as an invisible enabler, not the main focus.
Animations are slow and calming, and interactions are predictable and safe. This approach
supports long-term use and distinguishes Nuppu from fast-paced, addictive children’s apps.

Market Potential and Scalability
Children’s emotional and well-being skills are a rapidly growing field globally. Nuppu Digital is
positioned in the early stage where impact is greatest and customer loyalty is long-lasting.

A digital product enables scalability across languages, cultures, and usage environments. The
brand is built to endure and expand through new content, partnerships, and distribution
channels.

Meet the Nuppu Characters
Muru
Role: Safety &amp; Comfort
Emotional skills: Security, calming down, coping with disappointment
Muru is calm, gentle, and always ready to listen.
They remind children that all emotions are allowed.
With Muru, it’s okay to be quiet, sad, or tired—just the way you are.

Hippu
Role: Curiosity and practicing boundaries
Emotional skills: Expressing one’s own will, boundaries, courage
Hippu is a curious and independent cat who enjoys trying new things.
Hippu often wonders: What do I want? How do I feel?
Do I dare to say it out loud? Hippu teaches that personal boundaries
are important.

Nuppu
Role: Emotional guide and friendship
Emotional skills: Recognizing emotions, empathy, self-regulation
Nuppu is a gentle bunny who walks alongside
the child through all emotions. Nuppu can name feelings and
notices even the smallest details. Nuppu never
says a feeling is wrong, but helps the child understand
where it came from and where it can go.

Lumo
Role: Thinking and problem-solving
Emotional skills: Disappointment, conflict, flexibility, solutions
Lumo is a clever and perceptive fox who helps
examine situations from many angles.
Lumo doesn’t give ready-made answers but
asks good questions. From Lumo, children learn
that mistakes are part of learning—
and that even difficult emotions can teach us.
